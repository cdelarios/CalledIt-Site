Called It

Track the season with friends.

Called It is an iPhone app for awards season. Keep a list of the contenders, mark what you've seen, and see which of your friends have seen them too. When the nominations land, everyone calls their winners — and after the ceremony, the app scores who called it.

Invited by a friend?

Install Called It, then tap your friend's invite link again. You'll be connected the moment you're signed in.

Called It is in private testing. The App Store link will appear here when it's released.
